NexNote is a local-first note-taking app for Android.

It is built for fast personal notes without accounts, analytics, advertising, or proprietary runtime services.

Features:

* Write and edit notes with Markdown-oriented tools.
* Organize notes with tags and full-text search.
* Filter search by text scope and pinned state, then choose the result order.
* Browse notes by date in the agenda view.
* Create and reuse templates.
* Review local writing statistics, activity history, streaks, and tag insights.
* Move deleted notes to trash before permanent deletion.
* Select notes in bulk or delete all stored notes from Settings.
* Put selected notes and their images in a PIN-protected encrypted Vault.
* Export notes and share generated files through Android's system share flow.
* Customize theme, accent color, font scale, timezone, and left-handed layout preference.

Privacy:

* Notes are stored locally on the device.
* Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled; export data before uninstalling or changing devices.
* No account is required.
* No Firebase, Google Play Services, analytics, ads, or remote backend are included.
* Search and statistics indexes stay in the app's private on-device database.

NexNote keeps your notes on your Android device, without accounts, ads, analytics, or a remote server.

Write with Markdown tools and preview checklists, tables, images, and file attachments. Find notes with full-text search, tags, filters, and sorting. Browse Calendar and Agenda, reuse templates, and see your writing activity.

Features:

* Pin notes, select them for bulk actions, and recover deleted notes from trash.
* Keep selected notes, images, and documents in a PIN-protected encrypted Vault.
* Receive text and images shared from other apps, or open supported UTF-8 text files as new notes.
* Attach documents, audio, archives, and other files. Open ordinary-note attachments in an installed viewer.
* Export notes as text, Markdown, or PDF, or print them. Exports with document attachments can include a ZIP of the referenced files.
* Choose light, dark, OLED, or system appearance, nine accent colors, and optional Android 12+ device colors.
* Choose System, Inter, Lora, Fira Code, or JetBrains Mono text, and adjust the text size.

Privacy and backup:

* Notes, preferences, search indexes, and writing statistics stay on your device.
* Ordinary notes use Android's private app storage. Vault titles, text, file lists, and files have additional PIN-based encryption.
* Vault notes must be moved out of the Vault before exporting, printing, or opening attached files externally.
* Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled. Export files you want to keep before uninstalling or changing devices; exports are not a full app backup.
* Files shared with other apps are outside NexNote's control.

NexNote requires Android 10 or later and is free software licensed under GPL-3.0-only.
